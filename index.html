Good to know
before you go.

IN A PINCH maps restroom stops across the United States. Move the map to explore another area or open one of the 24 city guides.

Reading the atlas

Numbered groups show stops in a wider area. Zoom in to see individual places. Neighborhood searches load community-mapped restrooms, gas stations, rest areas, restaurants, bars and other places with possible bathroom access. Some areas have few mapped stops.

Checking access

“Mapped bathrooms” shows places with restroom evidence. The Showers filter shows stops where a source documents a shower. Check opening hours and fees with the operator. At venues marked “Bathroom unconfirmed,” ask staff about access. Pins may point to a building or park entrance. Directions open in Google Maps.

Reading the hours

“Open by schedule” uses the posted restroom hours in the facility’s time zone. Access and hours can change.

Finding nearby stops

Allow location access to center on nearby stops. You can also pan or choose a destination. Your location centers the map. Saved stops stay in this browser.

Your visit. Your verdict.

Leave an anonymous 1–5 star bathroom rating when the shared ratings service is available. No account or email needed. Ratings require a server deployment; a GitHub Pages upload supports maps, filters, saved stops and directions.
